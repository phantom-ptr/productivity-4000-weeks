                      Settings

                      With a birth date, the lifeline on the Focus page shows the weeks you have lived out of about 4,000. It stays in the database on this machine.

                      Focus holds 3 and Closed holds 10. These are fixed on purpose. Open holds 5 to 200.

                      Why it works this way

                      An average life is about four thousand weeks. You will never get through everything, so this app helps you choose a few things and let the rest go. It follows Oliver Burkeman's book Four Thousand Weeks.

                      Idea from the bookWhat the app does
                      Keep an open list and a closed list with a fixed size.Open holds 20 by default. Closed holds ten. When a list is full, its last item moves down a list.
                      Limit work in progress to about three.Focus holds three. To add a fourth, you choose which one steps aside.
                      Pay yourself first.One Focus item can be marked first. It sits alone at the top.
                      Decide in advance what to fail at. Resist middling priorities.The Neglect page. Each item set aside carries a one-line reason. A choice, not a failure.
                      The joy of missing out.Letting go is a real outcome, dated and in the report. Items in Open for two weeks show up on the Neglect page, where you can retire them.
                      Keep a done list.Every finish is kept with its date. Done today sits at the bottom of the Focus page. Nothing is ever deleted.
                      One big project at a time.An optional project tag. A quiet note appears when Focus spans more than one project.
                      Rest that does not have to earn its keep.Log something done "for its own sake", with no task behind it. Counted apart, never scored.
                      Small steady amounts. Stay on the bus.No streaks. A reminder to stop while you still want to go on.
                      Five questions worth asking.The weekly review shows one each week, with what you did, let go and neglected. No reminders.
                      About four thousand weeks.Optional: enter a birth date and the lifeline shows your weeks. Blank by default.
                      Efficiency and speed make things worse.No timers, no estimates, no due dates.
                      Boring, single-purpose tools.No accounts, no network, no notifications, no scores. Your data is one file on your machine.
